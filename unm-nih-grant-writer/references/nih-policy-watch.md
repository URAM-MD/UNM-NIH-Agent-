## 1. AI use and originality (NOT-OD-25-132)
- Applies to receipt dates on or after September 25, 2025.
- An application, or a section of one, that is substantially developed by AI is not considered the applicant's original work. NIH may use detection technology.
- If found after award, NIH may refer the matter to the Office of Research Integrity and may disallow costs, withhold future awards, suspend or terminate.
- NIH accepts AI assistance for limited aspects of preparation and warns about plagiarism and fabricated citations.
- Working implication for this skill: ideas, aims, data and argument come from the investigator. Editing, structuring, critique, compliance checking and tightening the investigator's own text are the appropriate uses. Never supply a citation you have not verified exists and says what is claimed.
- NIH has not published a numeric threshold for "substantially". Do not tell a user that a given amount of AI drafting is safe.

## 2. Annual application limit (NOT-OD-25-132)
- No more than six new, renewal, resubmission or revision applications per PD/PI or multiple PI across all council rounds in a calendar year.
- Covers all activity codes except T (training) codes and R13 conference grants.
- Ask a prolific PI how many they have already submitted this calendar year before planning another, and count MPI roles.

## 3. Biosketch, Other Support and Common Forms
- NIH adopted the federal Common Forms for the Biographical Sketch and for Current and Pending (Other) Support, plus an NIH Biographical Sketch Supplement (NOT-OD-26-018).
- Required for due dates on or after January 25, 2026. After a leniency period, eRA validation became a hard error on May 8, 2026 (NOT-OD-26-079). Applies to applications, Just-in-Time, RPPRs and prior approval requests.
- Old NIH biosketch and Other Support formats are rejected. Do not format a biosketch from memory of the pre-2026 layout; have the investigator generate it in SciENcv and help with the content (personal statement, contributions to science) rather than the form.
- Confirm current ORCID and SciENcv certification requirements on the NIH biosketch page before advising.

## 4. Research security training and foreign talent programs
- Senior/key personnel must certify research security training completed within the 12 months before the application date, for proposals on or after May 25, 2026 (NOT-OD-26-017). The representation is made in the biosketch in SciENcv.
- Anyone party to a malign foreign talent recruitment program is ineligible to be senior/key personnel; certification occurs at application and annually in the RPPR (NOT-OD-26-018).
- UNM's training platform and process: see `unm-data/institution/proposal-logistics.md`.

## 5. Letters of intent and the $500,000 rule (NOT-OD-26-019, December 2025)
- NIH no longer requests or accepts letters of intent.
- Prior agreement from an institute is no longer required for applications requesting $500,000 or more in direct costs in a single year, and the cover letter no longer needs to document it.
- Talking to a program officer before applying is still one of the most useful things an applicant can do; it is just not a formal gate.

## 6. Indirect (F&A) costs
- The 15% cap announced in NOT-OD-25-068 (February 2025) is not in effect. The First Circuit upheld the ruling against it on January 5, 2026 and the appeal window closed April 6, 2026.
- NOT-OD-26-072 (April 20, 2026): NIH applies the indirect cost provisions of 45 CFR Part 75 as required by FY2026 appropriations. Negotiated institutional rates apply. NIH withdrew two Uniform Guidance flexibilities: MTDC again includes only the first $25,000 of each subaward, and the de minimis rate for organizations without a negotiated rate is 10%. The $10,000 equipment threshold stays.
- Appropriations language is annual. Re-check this at the start of each federal fiscal year.
- UNM's own rates: `unm-data/institution/proposal-logistics.md`.

## 7. Peer review
- Simplified review framework (three factors) applies to most research project grant applications for due dates on or after January 25, 2025. Details in `nih-application-anatomy.md`.
- For the January 2026 and May 2026 councils NIH used modified practices after the 2025 lapse in appropriations: about 30 to 35 percent of applications discussed, a middle tier "competitive but not discussed" that remains eligible for funding, and shorter summary statements. Check whether this continues for the round the user is targeting, because it changes how to read a summary statement and whether "not discussed" means what it used to.

## 8. Re-check every time
- The forms version named in the NOFO and whether the NOFO has been reissued or expired. Many NOFOs were withdrawn or consolidated in 2025 and 2026; do not assume a familiar PA or PAR number is still open.
- Salary cap for the current fiscal year.
- Institute paylines and funding strategies (posted per institute, changed by appropriations).
- Data Management and Sharing Plan requirements and any institute-specific expectations.
- Rules on foreign components and foreign subawards, which were restructured in 2025.
- Inclusion, human subjects and clinical trial definitions if the study touches them.
