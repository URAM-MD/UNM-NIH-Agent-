# UNM College of Nursing

Stub created 2026-11-12. Official name is the College of Nursing. [CONFIRM]

Nursing applicants often target NINR, and also NIMHD, NIA, NICHD, NCI and AHRQ (AHRQ is not NIH and has its own rules). NINR's strategic framing emphasizes health equity, social determinants of health, population and community health, prevention and health promotion, and systems and models of care.
