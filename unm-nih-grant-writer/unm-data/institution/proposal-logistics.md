## F&A (indirect cost) rates
The most recent agreement found online is dated December 17, 2021 and covers July 1, 2021 through June 30, 2025. **It has passed its end date.** A newer or extended agreement almost certainly exists; get it from SPO before budgeting and replace this section. [NEEDED: current agreement]

From that 2021 agreement, base is modified total direct costs (MTDC) [VERIFIED 2026-11-12, hsc.unm.edu F&A federal approved rates memo]:
- On-campus research: 52.5% for 7/1/2022 to 6/30/2025 (51.5% for 7/1/2021 to 6/30/2022)
- On-campus instruction: 53%
- On-campus other sponsored activities: 45%
- Off-campus, all programs: 26%

NIH-side rules that affect the base are in the skill's `references/nih-policy-watch.md` (for example how much of each subaward counts toward MTDC).
