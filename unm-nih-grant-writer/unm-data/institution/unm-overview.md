## Identity and designations
- The University of New Mexico (UNM), Albuquerque, is New Mexico's flagship public research university. [CONFIRM]
- Carnegie classification: R1, very high research activity. [CONFIRM]
- Federally designated Hispanic-Serving Institution. [CONFIRM]
- New Mexico is an NIH IDeA (Institutional Development Award) state, which makes UNM eligible for NIGMS IDeA programs such as COBRE and INBRE. [CONFIRM]
- The UNM Comprehensive Cancer Center is an NCI-designated Comprehensive Cancer Center. [VERIFIED 2026-11-12, unmfirst.unm.edu facilities page]
